A-Alerts is the new name of P2000 Apeldoorn (same app, same bundle ID nl.lrvdlinden.p2000apeldoorn, update to 2.0.0). No account is required; every feature works without signing in. Sign in with Apple is optional and only syncs settings via iCloud.

WHAT THE APP DOES
It shows public P2000 alerts (the open pager network of the Dutch emergency services) for one municipality, Apeldoorn (incl. Teuge), with public NL-Alert, Burgernet and KNMI warnings and live trauma/police helicopters. Our own server (a Cloudflare Worker) collects and filters the data; the app only talks to that server. It is a local, single-purpose app by one developer, not a template or a copy of another app; it is the Apple version of the developer's own P2000 app for Homey (homey.app, nl.lrvdlinden.p2000).

HOW TO TEST
1. Open the app. The welcome screens set up a first notification profile (where, which alerts, warnings, quiet hours); every choice is optional, tap Next. Notifications and location are optional.
2. Pager: alerts of the last 24 hours, with NL-Alert/Burgernet between them when there are any. Tap an alert: map, Look Around, alerted stations with arrival time, timeline, weather, route and share buttons.
3. Map: chips at the top filter services, Burgernet and NL-Alert; Lifeliner/Zulu chips jump to the helicopter. The layers button switches stations, hospitals/air bases and helicopters.
4. Tap a helicopter: route (this flight / 30 min / 2 h), likely destination, recent flights and playback (play button).
5. Statistics: today versus yesterday.
6. In an alert, tap the star next to a capcode to follow that unit (Settings > Followed capcodes). The eye button in the pager hides viewed alerts. Settings > Help and FAQ. Settings > Notifications > Profiles; "Send a test notification" sends a push right away. Settings > Status shows the state of every component.
7. News tab (between Map and Statistics): police news, 112 Nieuws (title and intro, "Read more on 112nieuws.net" opens their site), weather articles and live blogs. Tap an item to read it; live blogs show a timeline. Filter chips at the top.
8. Apple Watch, Mac and Apple TV show the same data; Apple TV has a control-room view and a News tab.

REGION / DATA NOTES
Content is limited to one Dutch municipality. Alerts arrive several times per hour; warnings are rare, so lists can be empty. Helicopters only appear while they are tracked; outside a flight the route can be empty. This is expected, not an error.

DATA SOURCES
P2000 (public pager network), NL-Alert, Burgernet (incl. AMBER Alert and Missing Child Alert, public police calls), police news from the open politie.nl API (text with source credit, no police images), 112 Nieuws headlines and intros with written permission of 112 Nieuws Nederland (no photos from them), news images are royalty-free Pixabay photos, KNMI warnings, helicopter positions from open ADS-B data (adsb.lol, ODbL), stations/hospitals from OpenStreetMap (ODbL), weather from WeatherKit (Apple Weather mark and legal link shown). No affiliation with the emergency services or government is claimed; the in-app disclaimer says so.

PERMISSIONS
Notifications: to send alerts (welcome screen or Settings). Location (when in use): only for "My area" and distances; the app works fully without it.

IAP / ADS
No in-app purchases, subscriptions, ads or tracking.
