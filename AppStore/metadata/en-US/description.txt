A-Alerts (formerly P2000 Apeldoorn) shows live what happens in the municipality of Apeldoorn and Teuge in the Netherlands: alerts from the fire brigade, ambulance, police and trauma helicopter, together with NL-Alert, Burgernet and KNMI weather warnings.

LIVE PAGER
• Every P2000 alert for 26 towns and hamlets, grouped by day
• NL-Alert and Burgernet between the alerts, with the time of the warning
• A KNMI warning (code yellow, orange or red) as a bar at the top while it applies
• All warnings of the last two weeks
• Hide alerts you have already seen: when you are up to date you see "You're all caught up"

MAP WITH LAYERS AND FILTERS
• Filter with one tap on fire, ambulance, police, trauma helicopter, Burgernet and NL-Alert
• Switch on or off: KNMI areas, the border of the area, stations, hospitals and air bases
• Lifeliner 1 to 4 and the police helicopters live, moving smoothly, with heading, altitude and speed

LIFELINER AND POLICE HELICOPTER
• Route of this flight, the last 30 minutes or 2 hours
• Likely destination: the hospital or base it is heading for, with arrival time
• Play back flights at 30× to 300× speed
• A notification when a Lifeliner takes off for an alert in Apeldoorn

EVERY DETAIL
• Large map with Look Around and 3D
• Alerted stations with their route and an estimated arrival time
• The alert's Lifeliner live on the map, with its route
• Timeline of alerts that may belong to the same incident
• Capcodes, vehicles and the weather at the time of the alert
• Route via Apple Maps or Google Maps, and share with one tap

WARNINGS
• The same detail page for NL-Alert, Burgernet and KNMI: map with the area or place, the message and the weather
• KNMI as on knmi.nl: headline, description, risks and advice, and the course per code
• Live weather for a weather code; lifted warnings show code green, with the code that applied

NOTIFICATIONS
• Set up your own notification profile in a few steps on first launch
• Up to five notification profiles with their own places, services, priorities, warnings and times
• Follow a capcode with a star, for example your own fire station, also outside your places and radius
• KNMI: also a notification one hour before a code starts and when it is lifted
• No notification? The alert tells you why
• Notifications with a small map and buttons to save or open in Apple Maps
• My area: only alerts within a radius around you

EVERYWHERE
• iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Apple TV (control-room view)
• Widgets, iCloud sync of your settings and Face ID lock
• Status of every component in Settings and at a-alerts.nl/status
• Help and FAQ in the app and at a-alerts.nl/hulp

No account, no ads and no tracking. The app is available in Dutch and English.

A-Alerts is independent and not an official service of the emergency services or the government. Not every alert goes through P2000. In an emergency, always call 112.
